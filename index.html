Efficiency Scatter — Avg Talk Time vs Sale %

How to read this chart. Each dot is one agent. X = their average talk time (seconds). Y = their Sale conversion rate. Bubble size = total call volume. Dashed lines = team medians, dividing the chart into four quadrants:
Top-left (short talk, high sale %): efficient closers.
Top-right (long talk, high sale %): consultative closers.
Bottom-left (short talk, low sale %): may be dispositioning too fast without qualifying.
Bottom-right (long talk, low sale %): talking a lot without closing. This is the quadrant to worry about.
